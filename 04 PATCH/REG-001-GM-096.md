# REG-001 — GM-096 Patch

**Source:** GM Quality System Basics Overview Supplier Audit.pdf  
**Pages:** 224–225  
**Section:** 7.0 Layered Process Audits — Introduction  
**Patch:** GM-096

## New distinctions

### DIS-0517

**Layered Process Audits shall ensure consistent application and execution of standards.**

Status: **SOURCE-SUPPORTED**.

### DIS-0518

**Layered Process Audits are intended to improve built-in quality and increase operator/leadership awareness through coaching/teaching interaction between leadership and operators.**

Status: **SOURCE-SUPPORTED**.

### DIS-0519

**Layered Process Audits apply to assembly, manufacturing operations, shipping/receiving, all operations and other support functions.**

Status: **SOURCE-SUPPORTED**.

### DIS-0520

**Ownership of Layered Process Audits is assigned to the Plant / Operations Manager.**

Status: **SOURCE-SUPPORTED**.

### DIS-0521

**The LPA process explicitly includes development of high-risk items for auditing.**

Status: **SOURCE-SUPPORTED**.

### DIS-0522

**The LPA process is structured to include schedule/tracking, high-risk audit items, check-sheet concept, check-sheet evaluation, countermeasures and management review requirements.**

Status: **SOURCE-SUPPORTED**.

Note: this distinction records the explicit process decomposition in the source outline. It does not claim that these elements form a complete causal chain.

## Not added

The following are **not** entered as distinctions because pp. 224–225 do not yet define them:

- LPA frequency;
- layers / levels of leadership;
- high-risk selection criteria;
- checklist questions or scoring;
- countermeasure closure rules;
- escalation rules;
- management-review format.
