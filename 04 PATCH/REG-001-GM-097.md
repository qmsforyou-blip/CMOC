# REG-001 PATCH — GM-097 — Layered Process Audits pp. 226–227

## Извещение на изменение

**Извещение на изменение — REG-001 — 0119+300826**

### Источник
GM Quality System Basics Overview Supplier Audit.pdf, pp. 226–227, §7.1 Benefits; §7.2 Process explanation.

### Новые различения

**DIS-0523** — LPA provides a system to verify compliance to the documented process.  
**Статус:** SOURCE-SUPPORTED.

**DIS-0524** — LPA explicitly serves to instill discipline and improve communication.  
**Статус:** SOURCE-SUPPORTED.

**DIS-0525** — LPA ensures a high level of process control by identifying and controlling high-risk / significant process elements.  
**Статус:** SOURCE-SUPPORTED.

**DIS-0526** — LPA maintains proper application of standards through the operational readiness process.  
**Статус:** SOURCE-SUPPORTED.

**DIS-0527** — LPA identifies opportunities for improvement and provides a process for effective follow-up.  
**Статус:** SOURCE-SUPPORTED.

**DIS-0528** — LPA is a standardized audit performed on a regular, frequent basis.  
**Статус:** SOURCE-SUPPORTED.

**DIS-0529** — LPA is performed by all layers of the organization.  
**Статус:** SOURCE-SUPPORTED.

**DIS-0530** — LPA supplements ongoing control plan and job instruction checks.  
**Статус:** SOURCE-SUPPORTED.

**DIS-0531** — LPA is owned by manufacturing leadership; SOURCE identifies Team Leader through Plant / Operations Manager.  
**Статус:** SOURCE-SUPPORTED.

**DIS-0532** — Quality and other functions participate in and support the LPA system.  
**Статус:** SOURCE-SUPPORTED.

**DIS-0533** — SOURCE states that LPAs are an industry standard.  
**Статус:** SOURCE ASSERTION; not independently verified.

## Примечание по дедупликации

DIS-0528, DIS-0529 and DIS-0531 reinforce characteristics already introduced on p. 225, but p. 227 provides the substantive process definition and therefore records the distinctions here as explicit SOURCE statements. No additional CMOC interpretation is promoted to Canon from these pages.

## Boundary

Не извлекать из этих страниц конкретную периодичность для отдельных слоёв, правила формирования high-risk list, содержание check sheet, evaluation, countermeasure или management review. Эти элементы должны извлекаться из последующих страниц §7.2.
