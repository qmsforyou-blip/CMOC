# PATCH-ISO-025

## Извещение на изменение

**0094+270826**

### SOURCE
ISO/DIS 9001:2025(en)

### LOCATION
Clause 7.2 — Competence; Annex A.7.2

### SOURCE CLAIM

The organization shall determine the necessary competence of persons doing work under its control that affects its quality management system performance. It shall ensure that these persons are competent on the basis of appropriate education, training, or experience. Where applicable, the organization shall take actions to acquire the necessary competence and evaluate the effectiveness of the actions taken. Appropriate documented information shall be available as evidence of competence. Applicable actions can include training, mentoring, reassignment of currently employed persons, or hiring/contracting competent persons. fileciteturn64file8

Annex A states that competence needed for performance and effectiveness of the quality system should be determined and addressed at all levels of the organizational structure, starting with top management. Necessary competence can be obtained through recruitment, assigning different roles, career progression, or training. Competence management, including acquisition and transfer of knowledge and experience, enhances organizational knowledge. For externally provided processes, products and services, the organization can need to determine and communicate competence requirements, including qualifications. Competences may need regular review or review when changes occur, with subsequent actions to ensure continuing suitability. fileciteturn64file12

### DISTINCTIONS

**DIS-001 — COMPETENCE ≠ EDUCATION / TRAINING / EXPERIENCE**

Education, training and experience are bases on which competence is ensured; they are not identified as competence itself.

**STATUS: NEW / SINGLE-SOURCE**

**DIS-002 — NECESSARY COMPETENCE ≠ COMPETENCE IN GENERAL**

The organization determines competence necessary for persons whose work affects QMS performance.

**STATUS: NEW / SINGLE-SOURCE**

**DIS-003 — ACQUIRING COMPETENCE ≠ ENSURING COMPETENCE**

Acquiring necessary competence is an action taken where applicable; ensuring that persons are competent is the resulting requirement.

**STATUS: NEW / SINGLE-SOURCE**

**DIS-004 — ACTION ≠ EFFECTIVENESS OF ACTION**

The organization may take actions to acquire competence and shall evaluate the effectiveness of those actions.

**STATUS: NEW / SINGLE-SOURCE**

**DIS-005 — COMPETENCE ≠ QUALIFICATION**

Qualification is mentioned as potentially required, including for external providers, but is not equated with competence.

**STATUS: NEW / SINGLE-SOURCE**

**DIS-006 — COMPETENCE ≠ PERSON / ROLE**

Competence belongs to persons; Annex A also identifies assigning different roles as one means by which necessary competence can be obtained. Neither person nor role is identical to competence.

**STATUS: NEW / SINGLE-SOURCE**

**DIS-007 — COMPETENCE ≠ ORGANIZATIONAL KNOWLEDGE**

Competence management can enhance organizational knowledge through acquisition and transfer of knowledge and experience, but the Source does not identify competence and organizational knowledge as the same entity.

**STATUS: NEW / SINGLE-SOURCE**

**DIS-008 — COMPETENCE ≠ FIXED STATE**

Annex A indicates that competences may need review regularly or when changes occur, followed by actions to ensure continuing suitability.

**STATUS: NEW / SINGLE-SOURCE**

**DIS-009 — EVIDENCE OF COMPETENCE ≠ COMPETENCE ITSELF**

Documented information is required as evidence of competence; the evidence is not the competence itself.

**STATUS: NEW / SINGLE-SOURCE**

**GM-002**

> Не принимай education, training или experience за саму competence: это основания её обеспечения.

**GM-003**

> Если competence недостаточна, различай действие по её приобретению и оценку effectiveness этого действия.

**GM-004**

> Рассматривай competence как изменяемое состояние: при изменениях и с течением времени её suitability может потребовать повторной оценки.

### CORE CANDIDATES

**CORE-CANDIDATE-044 — Competence is function-dependent**

Necessary competence is determined from the work performed and its effect on QMS performance, rather than from a generic list of qualifications.

**STATUS: STRONG CANDIDATE / SINGLE-SOURCE**

**CORE-CANDIDATE-045 — Competence must be demonstrated by evidence, not replaced by evidence**

Documented information is evidence of competence; it does not constitute competence itself.

**STATUS: STRONG CANDIDATE / SINGLE-SOURCE**

**CORE-CANDIDATE-046 — Competence management is a closed control loop**

The Source links determination of competence, action to acquire it where necessary, and evaluation of action effectiveness.

**STATUS: STRONG CANDIDATE / SINGLE-SOURCE**

**CORE-CANDIDATE-047 — Competence can contribute to organizational knowledge**

Competence management, through acquisition and transfer of knowledge and experience, enhances organizational knowledge.

**STATUS: CANDIDATE / SINGLE-SOURCE**

### DOCUMENT / DOCUMENTED INFORMATION / RECORD

**Documented information is explicitly required as evidence of competence.** fileciteturn64file8

```text
COMPETENCE
      ↓

      ↓

```

The Source does not here equate this documented information with a `record`. The Document / Record distinction remains open for Clause 7.5.

### CMOC INTERPRETATION

ISO-025 gives a sharper structure than ISO-024:

```text
FUNCTION / WORK
        ↓
REQUIRED COMPETENCE
        ↓
CURRENT COMPETENCE
        ↓
GAP
        ↓
ACTION
        ↓
EFFECTIVENESS
        ↓
COMPETENCE
```

The crucial point is that the control loop does not stop at “training performed”. ISO requires evaluation of the effectiveness of actions taken to acquire competence.

A second important relation is:

```text
COMPETENCE MANAGEMENT
        ↓
KNOWLEDGE + EXPERIENCE
        ↓
ORGANIZATIONAL KNOWLEDGE
```

This is a Source-supported relation, while the exact CMOC ontology remains a candidate.

### CROSS-CLAUSE OBSERVATION

ISO-024 established organizational knowledge as a managed resource. ISO-025 now distinguishes competence from knowledge while explicitly linking competence management to acquisition and transfer of knowledge and experience.

A strong multi-source candidate emerges:

```text
FUNCTION / PROCESS NEED
        ↓
REQUIRED KNOWLEDGE / COMPETENCE
        ↓
PERSON / ROLE
        ↓
ACTION TO CLOSE GAP
        ↓
EVALUATION
        ↓
RESULTING COMPETENCE / KNOWLEDGE
```

This is deliberately not promoted to Chain or Core: the complete composition is a CMOC interpretation assembled from adjacent structures, not a sequence explicitly stated by the Source.
