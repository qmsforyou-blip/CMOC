# REG-001 PATCH — GM-098 — Layered Process Audits pp. 228–229

## Извещение на изменение

**Извещение на изменение — REG-001 — 0120+300826**

### Новые различения

**DIS-0534** — LPA is a system comprising schedule/tracking, high-risk item identification, checklist evaluation, corrective action/countermeasures and senior-management review.  
**Статус:** SOURCE-SUPPORTED.

**DIS-0535** — The workstation to be audited is selected based on the LPA schedule.  
**Статус:** SOURCE-SUPPORTED.

**DIS-0536** — The LPA Check sheet evaluates the current process against established standards.  
**Статус:** SOURCE-SUPPORTED.

**DIS-0537** — Audit results are immediately communicated to all Team members.  
**Статус:** SOURCE-SUPPORTED.

**DIS-0538** — All deviations are recorded on the LPA Check sheet and Countermeasure sheet.  
**Статус:** SOURCE-SUPPORTED.

**DIS-0539** — A target close date and champion are assigned to the action.  
**Статус:** SOURCE-SUPPORTED.

**DIS-0540** — Suggested countermeasures are to be implemented as soon as possible.  
**Статус:** SOURCE-SUPPORTED.

**DIS-0541** — Open items are followed up to closure by the target close date.  
**Статус:** SOURCE-SUPPORTED.

**DIS-0542** — A problem is elevated to a higher level after the target close date.  
**Статус:** SOURCE-SUPPORTED.

**DIS-0543** — Management review is part of the LPA follow-up flow.  
**Статус:** SOURCE-SUPPORTED.

**DIS-0544** — LPA connects process verification with corrective action/countermeasures, ownership, due date, follow-up and escalation.  
**Статус:** SOURCE-SUPPORTED synthesis of the explicit sequence on p.229.

## CMOC candidate

**CAND-0545** — LPA can be represented as a recurring control loop: schedule → workstation → audit → check → deviation → documentation → owner/date → countermeasure → follow-up → escalation → management review.

**Статус:** CMOC INTERPRETATION / CANDIDATE; not a direct SOURCE formulation.

## Boundary

Pages 228–229 establish the components and operating sequence but do not yet specify detailed rules for schedule construction, high-risk selection, checklist evaluation, countermeasure acceptance or closure criteria. Do not canonize those rules until their source pages are processed.
