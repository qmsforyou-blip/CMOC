### LOCATION
Clause 5.1 — Leadership and commitment; 5.1.1 General; 5.1.2 Customer focus

### SOURCE CLAIM

Top management shall demonstrate leadership and commitment with respect to the quality management system by ensuring compatibility of quality policy and quality objectives with the organization's strategic direction; integration of QMS requirements into business processes; availability of resources; communication of the importance of effective quality management and conformity to QMS requirements; achievement of intended results; direction and support of persons contributing to QMS effectiveness; continual improvement; support for relevant roles to demonstrate leadership in their areas of responsibility; promotion of quality culture and ethical behaviour; promotion of process approach and risk-based thinking; and accountability for QMS effectiveness. fileciteturn46file7

For customer focus, top management shall ensure that customer and applicable statutory/regulatory requirements are determined, understood and consistently met; risks and opportunities affecting conformity and the ability to enhance customer satisfaction are determined and addressed; and focus on enhancing customer satisfaction is maintained. fileciteturn46file7

Annex A.5.1 states that visible support, involvement and commitment of top management are necessary for effective QMS implementation. It also states that ethical behaviour is part of quality culture and fundamental to leadership commitment. Top management's commitment is described as ensuring conformity consistently, i.e. systematically and continually, and enhancing customer satisfaction. fileciteturn46file0

### DISTINCTIONS

**DIS-001 — RESPONSIBILITY ≠ ACCOUNTABILITY**

The Source's terminology guidance states that responsibility for an activity can be delegated, but accountability for the results cannot be delegated. Clause 5.1 assigns top management accountability for QMS effectiveness. fileciteturn46file18turn46file7

**DIS-002 — QMS REQUIREMENTS ≠ SEPARATE QUALITY ACTIVITY**

**DIS-003 — LEADERSHIP ≠ SUPPORT ONLY**

Leadership and commitment are expressed through a set of management actions and accountability, not merely through visible support. Annex A.5.1 nevertheless identifies visible support, involvement and commitment as necessary for effective implementation. fileciteturn46file0

**STATUS: CANDIDATE / 1 source**

**DIS-004 — CUSTOMER FOCUS ≠ CUSTOMER SATISFACTION ONLY**

Customer focus includes determining and understanding requirements, consistently meeting them, addressing relevant risks and opportunities, and maintaining focus on enhancing satisfaction. fileciteturn46file7

### DOCUMENT / DOCUMENTED INFORMATION / RECORD — SPECIAL OBSERVATION

Clause 5.1 itself does **not** establish a new requirement specifically about document, documented information or record. Therefore no new distinction is extracted from 5.1 on that basis.

However, the Source's terminology guidance gives an important cross-clause confirmation of the historical distinction: "shall be available as documented information" replaces the former "maintain documented information", which previously referred to documentation other than records; and "documented information shall be available as evidence of" replaces the former "retain documented information as evidence of", which previously referred to records. The Source states that "as evidence of" indicates objective evidence to be retained. fileciteturn46file18

Therefore the CMOC investigation retains:

```text
DOCUMENT
      ≠
DOCUMENTED INFORMATION
      ≠
RECORD
```

as a critical cross-clause distinction, but **ISO-013 does not itself close or canonize it**. Clause 7.5 will be the principal extraction point.

### CMOC INTERPRETATION

ISO-013 changes the architectural viewpoint from the structure of the QMS to the actor accountable for its effectiveness.

```text
STRATEGIC DIRECTION
        ↓
QUALITY POLICY + OBJECTIVES
        ↓
QMS REQUIREMENTS
        ↓
BUSINESS PROCESSES
        ↓
RESOURCES + PEOPLE + SUPPORT
        ↓
INTENDED RESULTS
        ↓
QMS EFFECTIVENESS
        ↑
ACCOUNTABILITY OF TOP MANAGEMENT
```

The important CMOC interpretation is not merely that leadership supports people. It is that **system effectiveness has an accountable organizational subject**, while operational responsibilities may be distributed.
