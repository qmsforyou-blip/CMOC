# PATCH-ISO-019

## Извещение на изменение

**0088+270826**

### SOURCE
ISO/DIS 9001:2025(en)

### LOCATION
Clause 7.1 — Resources; 7.1.1 General; 7.1.2 People; 7.1.3 Infrastructure; 7.1.4 Environment for the operation of processes; 7.1.5 Monitoring and measuring resources; 7.1.6 Organizational knowledge

### SOURCE CLAIM

The organization shall determine and provide the resources needed for establishment, implementation, maintenance and continual improvement of the QMS, considering capabilities and constraints of existing internal resources and what needs to be obtained from external providers. fileciteturn57file0

The organization shall determine and provide people necessary for effective QMS implementation and operation/control of processes; infrastructure necessary for process operation and conformity; and the environment necessary for process operation and conformity. Infrastructure can include buildings/utilities, equipment including hardware/software, transportation and ICT. The operating environment can include social, psychological and physical factors. fileciteturn57file0

Monitoring and measuring resources shall be determined and provided to ensure valid and reliable results when monitoring/measuring is used to verify conformity. Such resources shall be suitable for their intended activities and maintained for continuing fitness for purpose. Appropriate documented information shall be available as evidence of fitness for purpose. Where measurement traceability is required or considered essential for confidence in validity, equipment shall be calibrated/verified as specified, identified for calibration/verification status and safeguarded. If equipment is found unfit, the organization shall determine whether previous results were adversely affected and take appropriate action. fileciteturn57file0

The organization shall determine knowledge necessary for process operation and intended QMS results. Knowledge shall be retained, applied and shared to the extent necessary. When needs and trends change, the organization shall consider current knowledge and determine how to acquire/access additional knowledge and required updates. fileciteturn57file0

Annex A.7.1 adds that determining/providing resources means determining what is necessary and appropriate for QMS effectiveness; activities require people, tools, environment, skills, monitoring/measuring resources and knowledge, and all components and their interactions should be managed to ensure continued suitability. fileciteturn57file8

### DISTINCTIONS

**DIS-001 — RESOURCE ≠ SINGLE RESOURCE TYPE**

Clause 7.1 treats resources as a set that includes people, infrastructure, operating environment, monitoring/measuring resources and organizational knowledge. Annex A.7.1 explicitly describes these components and their interactions. fileciteturn57file8

**STATUS: CANDIDATE / SINGLE-SOURCE**

**DIS-002 — INTERNAL RESOURCE ≠ EXTERNAL RESOURCE**

The organization considers capabilities/constraints of existing internal resources and what needs to be obtained from external providers. fileciteturn57file0

**STATUS: CANDIDATE / SINGLE-SOURCE**

**DIS-003 — MONITORING ≠ MEASUREMENT**

Annex A.7.1.5 distinguishes monitoring as determination of a status through observing/supervising/keeping under review, while measurement determines a value. The same equipment can, depending on circumstances, serve indication, monitoring or measuring purposes. fileciteturn56file2

**STATUS: CANDIDATE / SINGLE-SOURCE**

**DIS-004 — VALID RESULT ≠ RELIABLE RESULT**

7.1.5 explicitly requires resources to ensure valid and reliable results. The Source does not equate the two terms.

**STATUS: CANDIDATE / SINGLE-SOURCE**

**DIS-005 — SUITABLE RESOURCE ≠ FIT FOR PURPOSE**

Resources must be suitable for the specific monitoring/measuring activity and maintained for continuing fitness for purpose. Suitability and continuing fitness are separate requirements/states. fileciteturn57file0

**STATUS: CANDIDATE / SINGLE-SOURCE**

**DIS-006 — KNOWLEDGE ≠ KNOWLEDGE MANAGEMENT**

The clause requires knowledge to be determined, retained, applied and shared, and additional knowledge/access to be addressed when needs and trends change. The Source therefore describes management of knowledge, not merely possession of knowledge. fileciteturn57file0

**STATUS: CANDIDATE / SINGLE-SOURCE**

**DIS-007 — PROVIDING A RESOURCE ≠ ENSURING CONTINUED SUITABILITY**

Annex A.7.1 states that components and their interactions should be managed to ensure continued suitability; 7.1.5 explicitly requires maintenance for continuing fitness for purpose. fileciteturn57file8

**STATUS: CANDIDATE / SINGLE-SOURCE**

### GM

**GM-001**

> Рассматривай ресурс не как приобретённый объект, а как управляемый компонент системы: определить потребность → обеспечить → поддерживать пригодность во времени.

**GM-002**

> Для monitoring/measuring ресурса контролируй не сам факт наличия средства, а пригодность получать valid and reliable results.

**GM-003**

> Organizational knowledge должно не просто существовать: его необходимо retain, apply и share, а при изменении потребностей — обновлять или получать.

### CORE CANDIDATE

**CORE-CANDIDATE-029 — Resource is a managed system component**

Resources are not merely things provided. The Source requires determination, provision and, where applicable, maintenance/suitability, while Annex A.7.1 emphasizes management of components and their interactions for continued suitability. fileciteturn57file8

**STATUS: STRONG CANDIDATE / SINGLE-SOURCE**

**CORE-CANDIDATE-030 — Resource adequacy is relative to intended use/result**

The adequacy of a resource is tied to process operation, conformity, intended results or fitness for purpose rather than to its existence alone.

**STATUS: CANDIDATE / SINGLE-SOURCE**

**CORE-CANDIDATE-031 — Organizational knowledge is an active system resource**

Necessary knowledge must be retained, applied and shared and renewed as needs/trends change; it is therefore treated as an active resource of the management system rather than merely stored information.

**STATUS: STRONG CANDIDATE / SINGLE-SOURCE**

### DOCUMENT / DOCUMENTED INFORMATION / RECORD

This clause gives explicit documented-information requirements in the measurement-resource domain:

```text
MONITORING / MEASURING RESOURCE
        ↓
FITNESS FOR PURPOSE
        ↓
DOCUMENTED INFORMATION
        ↓
EVIDENCE
```

and, where no international/national measurement standard exists:

```text
CALIBRATION / VERIFICATION BASIS
        ↓
AVAILABLE AS DOCUMENTED INFORMATION
```

fileciteturn57file0

We deliberately do not call either artefact a "record". The Source uses `documented information` and separately uses `as evidence` as a function. This is a strong new data point for the Document / Documented information / Record investigation, but Clause 7.5 remains the principal place for the ontology of documented information.

### CMOC INTERPRETATION

7.1 gives a potentially important architectural definition of what a system component called a **resource** means in operation:

```text
SYSTEM / PROCESS NEED
        ↓
RESOURCE
        ↓
CAPABILITY / SUITABILITY
        ↓
PROCESS OPERATION
        ↓
RESULT
```

The resource is therefore not simply an object available to the organization. Its management includes determining whether it is needed, providing it, considering constraints, maintaining suitability and ensuring that its use supports intended results.

A second strong structure appears for organizational knowledge:

```text
KNOWLEDGE
 ↓
RETAIN
 ↓
APPLY
 ↓
SHARE
 ↓
UPDATE / ACQUIRE
```

This is particularly relevant to CMOC because knowledge is explicitly connected to process operation and intended QMS results, while loss or obsolescence of knowledge becomes a management concern. The Source also recognizes experience-based knowledge, lessons learned, tacit and undocumented knowledge, process/product/service improvements, learning from customers/providers, standards, research, conferences and intellectual property as possible sources of organizational knowledge. fileciteturn56file4

A third important structure is measurement validity:

```text
MEASUREMENT
 ↓
RESOURCE
 ↓
VALIDITY / RELIABILITY
 ↓
RESULT
```

and if the resource is found unfit:

```text
UNFIT RESOURCE
 ↓
QUESTION PREVIOUS RESULTS
 ↓
ACTION
```

This is a direct bridge toward the CMOC concepts of evidence, status, evaluation and corrective action, but those relations are not closed here.
