# PATCH-ISO-026

## Извещение на изменение

**0095+270826**

### SOURCE
ISO/DIS 9001:2025(en)

### LOCATION
Clause 7.3 — Awareness; Annex A.7.3

### SOURCE CLAIM

The organization shall ensure that persons doing work under its control are aware of: the quality policy; their contribution to the effectiveness of the quality management system, including the benefits of improved performance; the implications of not conforming with quality management system requirements; relevant quality objectives; and organizational quality culture and ethical behaviour. fileciteturn68file8

Annex A explains that awareness is attained when people understand their responsibilities and authorities and how their actions contribute to achievement of quality objectives. Awareness can be demonstrated through day-to-day activities: distinguishing what is acceptable and what is not, and taking appropriate action when processes, products or services do not meet agreed specifications. It is not enough for people to be aware; they need to apply this awareness in their work. Awareness includes understanding one's place in the process, applying customer focus, understanding the consequences of nonconformity on subsequent or related activities and customer requirements, and knowing what needs to be achieved by one's work. fileciteturn68file0

### DISTINCTIONS

**DIS-001 — AWARENESS ≠ KNOWLEDGE OF INFORMATION**

The Annex describes awareness through understanding responsibilities/authorities, contribution, and application in work. It is not merely possession of information.

**STATUS: NEW / SINGLE-SOURCE**

**DIS-002 — AWARENESS ≠ COMPETENCE**

Competence concerns the ability to apply knowledge and skills to achieve intended results; awareness concerns understanding and applying one's responsibilities, contribution, objectives, requirements and consequences. The Source treats them in separate clauses.

**STATUS: NEW / SINGLE-SOURCE**

**DIS-003 — AWARENESS ≠ COMMUNICATION**

Communication can support awareness, but 7.3 specifies awareness as a state to be ensured in persons, while 7.4 separately addresses communication.

**STATUS: CANDIDATE / SINGLE-SOURCE**

**DIS-004 — AWARENESS ≠ UNDERSTANDING ONLY**

Annex A explicitly states that people need not only to be aware but also to apply this awareness in their work.

**STATUS: NEW / SINGLE-SOURCE**

**DIS-005 — AWARENESS ≠ ABSTRACT QUALITY UNDERSTANDING**

Awareness is connected to concrete work: responsibilities, authorities, contribution, acceptable/not acceptable conditions, actions on nonconformity and what the person's work needs to achieve.

**STATUS: NEW / SINGLE-SOURCE**

**DIS-006 — PERSONAL CONTRIBUTION ≠ ORGANIZATIONAL OBJECTIVE**

A person is to understand how their actions contribute to quality objectives and what needs to be achieved by their own work; the personal contribution is not identical to the organizational objective.

**STATUS: NEW / SINGLE-SOURCE**

**DIS-007 — NONCONFORMITY AWARENESS ≠ NONCONFORMITY MANAGEMENT**

Awareness includes understanding implications and knowing appropriate action, but 7.3 does not itself prescribe the full nonconformity management mechanism.

**STATUS: NEW / SINGLE-SOURCE**

### GM

**GM-001**

> Проверяй awareness не вопросом «знает ли человек?», а по тому, понимает ли он свою ответственность, contribution, objectives и consequences и применяет ли это в работе.

**GM-002**

> Awareness должна доходить до различения acceptable / not acceptable и соответствующего действия.

**GM-003**

> Связывай awareness человека с его местом в процессе и с тем, что именно должно быть достигнуто его работой.

**GM-004**

> При оценке awareness учитывай последствия nonconformity не только для текущей операции, но и для последующих/связанных действий и customer requirements.

### CAPABILITY

CMOC interpretation: ability of persons to understand their role, responsibilities, contribution and objectives and translate that awareness into appropriate day-to-day action, including response to nonconformity.

**STATUS: CANDIDATE / SINGLE-SOURCE**

### CMOC INTERPRETATION

ISO-026 gives awareness a distinctly operational character:

```text
ROLE / RESPONSIBILITY
        ↓
UNDERSTANDING
        ↓
PERSONAL CONTRIBUTION
        ↓
RECOGNITION OF STATE
        ↓
ACTION
```

The key CMOC interpretation is:

> **Awareness is not merely possession of information; it becomes observable when a person uses understanding of role, objectives, requirements and consequences to distinguish a relevant state and act appropriately.**

This is CMOC interpretation, not an ISO definition.

A second important structure is:

```text
PERSON
 ↓
PLACE IN PROCESS
 ↓
CONTRIBUTION
 ↓
SYSTEM OBJECTIVE
```

This provides a potentially important bridge between `ROLE`, `PROCESS`, `OBJECTIVE` and `ACTION`, but it remains a candidate until independently confirmed.

### CROSS-CLAUSE OBSERVATION

ISO-024: organizational knowledge is maintained and made available.

ISO-025: competence is the ability to apply knowledge and skills to achieve intended results; competence is ensured and its effectiveness evaluated.

ISO-026: awareness connects a person's responsibilities, contribution, objectives and understanding of consequences with day-to-day action.

A useful candidate distinction now emerges:

```text
KNOWLEDGE
   ↓
COMPETENCE
   ↓
AWARENESS
   ↓
ACTION
```

However, this complete linear chain is **CMOC interpretation**, not a sequence explicitly stated by ISO. Do not promote it to Chain or Core yet.
