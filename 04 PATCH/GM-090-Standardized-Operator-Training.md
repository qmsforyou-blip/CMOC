## 8. CMOC INTERPRETATION

This fragment materially extends the previous Standardized Work block.

Previously, SOURCE established that organizations shall train impacted and new employees in the use of Standardized Work Instructions and maintain/update those instructions as processes/parts change.

Now SOURCE opens a dedicated training system and immediately frames it through **verification and documentation**. This suggests that training is not merely an activity but may have a controlled evidence structure.

However, this is still an interpretation. The pages shown do not yet tell us:

- what constitutes successful verification;
- who verifies it;
- what exactly is documented;
- when certification occurs;
- how records are maintained;
- what happens after a process or instruction changes.

Those questions remain for the next SOURCE fragments.
