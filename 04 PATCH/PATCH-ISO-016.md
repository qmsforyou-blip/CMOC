# PATCH-ISO-016

## Извещение на изменение

**0085+270826**

### SOURCE
ISO/DIS 9001:2025(en)

### LOCATION
Clause 6.1 — Actions to address risks and opportunities; 6.1.1; 6.1.2

### SOURCE CLAIM

When planning the QMS, the organization shall consider the issues referred to in 4.1 and the requirements referred to in 4.2 and determine the risks and opportunities that need to be addressed to give assurance that the QMS can achieve its intended results; enhance desirable effects; prevent, or reduce, undesirable effects; and achieve improvement. It shall plan actions to address these risks and opportunities, how to integrate and implement the actions into QMS processes, and how to evaluate the effectiveness of those actions. Actions shall be proportionate to the potential impact on conformity of products and services. fileciteturn43file2

The Source does not prescribe a specific documented procedure or formal risk-management process. It requires determination of risks and opportunities, planning of actions, integration and implementation into QMS processes, evaluation of action effectiveness, and proportionality. fileciteturn42file0

### DISTINCTIONS

**DIS-001 — RISK ≠ OPPORTUNITY**

The clause requires risks and opportunities to be determined separately as matters needing action. Risk was defined in Clause 3 as the effect of uncertainty; opportunity is associated with a potential desirable effect. fileciteturn43file2

**STATUS: CONFIRM / UPDATE — ISO-008 + ISO-016**

**DIS-002 — RISK ≠ ONLY NEGATIVE EVENT**

Risk is an effect of uncertainty and therefore is not limited by the Source's definition to a negative event. fileciteturn43file2

**STATUS: CONFIRM — ISO-008; operationally applied in ISO-016**

**DIS-003 — DETERMINATION ≠ ACTION**

Identifying risks and opportunities is distinct from planning actions to address them.

**STATUS: CANDIDATE / 1 source**

**DIS-004 — PLANNED ACTION ≠ IMPLEMENTED ACTION**

The organization must plan actions and determine how to integrate and implement them into QMS processes. Planning and implementation are therefore distinct stages.

**STATUS: CANDIDATE / 1 source**

**DIS-005 — ACTION ≠ EFFECTIVE ACTION**

The effectiveness of actions must be evaluated; taking an action does not by itself establish that it was effective. fileciteturn43file2

**STATUS: CANDIDATE / 1 source**

**DIS-006 — PROPORTIONALITY ≠ UNIFORM ACTION**

Actions are to be proportionate to the potential impact on conformity of products and services. The Source therefore does not prescribe identical treatment for all risks/opportunities. fileciteturn43file2

**STATUS: CANDIDATE / 1 source**

### CORE CANDIDATE

**CORE-CANDIDATE-022 — Action is part of the managed process**

A risk/opportunity response is not treated as a parallel list of measures: the action is to be integrated and implemented into QMS processes and its effectiveness evaluated.

**STATUS: STRONG CANDIDATE / SINGLE-SOURCE**

**CORE-CANDIDATE-023 — Effectiveness closes the action loop**

Planning and implementing an action does not close the management loop; effectiveness must be evaluated.

**STATUS: CANDIDATE / SINGLE-SOURCE**

### CMOC INTERPRETATION

ISO-016 extends the architecture developed in 4.1–4.4:

```text
CONTEXT / ISSUES
        +
INTERESTED-PARTY REQUIREMENTS
        ↓
RISKS + OPPORTUNITIES
        ↓
ACTIONS
        ↓
INTEGRATION INTO PROCESSES
        ↓
IMPLEMENTATION
        ↓
EFFECTIVENESS EVALUATION
```

The important CMOC interpretation is:

> **A risk/opportunity response becomes part of the management system when it is integrated into the processes of the system and its effectiveness is evaluated.**

This is not merely a risk register logic. It is an integration logic.

A second important point is proportionality: the Source connects the scale of the action to the potential impact on conformity. This is a decision rule, not a specific risk methodology.
