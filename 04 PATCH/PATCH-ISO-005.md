# PATCH-ISO-005

## Извещение на изменение

**0074+250826**

### SOURCE
ISO/DIS 9001:2025(en)

### LOCATION
Introduction, 0.4 — Relationship with other management system standards

### SOURCE CLAIM

The document applies a harmonized structure to improve alignment among ISO management system standards.

It enables an organization to use the process approach, coupled with the PDCA cycle, risk-based thinking and opportunity-based thinking, to align or integrate its quality management system with requirements of other management system standards.

The document relates to ISO/CD 9000 as essential background for understanding and implementation, ISO 9004 as guidance for organizations progressing beyond the requirements, and ISO/TS 9002 as implementation guidance.

The document does not include requirements specific to other management systems such as environmental, occupational health and safety, or financial management. Sector-specific ISO quality management system standards may add requirements or provide sector-specific guidance.

### DISTINCTIONS

**DIS-001 — QMS requirements ≠ requirements of other management systems**

ISO 9001 can align or integrate with other management system requirements, but does not absorb their specific requirements into the QMS requirements.

**STATUS: CANDIDATE / 1 source**

**DIS-002 — ALIGNMENT ≠ INTEGRATION**

The Source names alignment and integration as related possibilities, without defining them as identical operations.

**STATUS: CANDIDATE / 1 source**

**DIS-003 — STANDARD STRUCTURE ≠ ORGANIZATIONAL SYSTEM**

The harmonized structure is a means of improving alignment among standards; it does not constitute the organization's integrated management system itself.

**STATUS: CANDIDATE / 1 source**

**DIS-004 — REQUIREMENTS ≠ GUIDANCE**

ISO/CD 9000, ISO 9004 and ISO/TS 9002 have different stated functions relative to this document: background, guidance beyond requirements, and implementation guidance.

**STATUS: CANDIDATE / 1 source**

### CMOC INTERPRETATION

The significant CMOC extraction is not "ISO standards can be combined". It is the stronger architectural proposition that **different management systems can be composed around a common management architecture while preserving system-specific requirements**.

This extends the ISO-003 / ISO-004 structure:

```text
PROCESS APPROACH
      ↓
PDCA / RISK / OPPORTUNITY
      ↓
MANAGEMENT ARCHITECTURE
      ↓
ALIGNMENT / INTEGRATION
      ↓
MULTIPLE MANAGEMENT SYSTEM REQUIREMENTS
```

This is a CMOC interpretation, not a direct ISO definition.
