**0089+270826**

### SOURCE
ISO/DIS 9001:2025(en)

### LOCATION
Clause 7.1.2 — People; Annex A.7.1.2

### SOURCE CLAIM

The organization shall determine and provide the persons necessary for the effective implementation of its quality management system and for the operation and control of its processes. fileciteturn59file8

Annex A.7.1.2 explains that required workforce should be determined based on expected workload, ensuring necessary people by number and organizational knowledge. Insufficient people in relevant roles can limit the QMS's ability to achieve its objectives and can be addressed by risk-based thinking. Necessary people can be external to the organization and collaborate only temporarily. Emerging technologies can be used in place of people for some functions; where this is done, related risks and opportunities should be carefully evaluated, including implications for ethical behaviour and accountability. fileciteturn59file5

### DISTINCTIONS

**DIS-001 — PEOPLE AS RESOURCE ≠ PEOPLE AS HEADCOUNT**

The Source requires persons necessary for QMS effectiveness and process operation/control. Annex A.7.1.2 adds number and organizational knowledge as dimensions of required workforce.

**STATUS: CANDIDATE / SINGLE-SOURCE**

**DIS-002 — REQUIRED PEOPLE ≠ FIXED STAFF**

Necessary people can be external to the organization and collaborate temporarily.

**STATUS: NEW / SINGLE-SOURCE**

**DIS-003 — NUMBER OF PEOPLE ≠ ORGANIZATIONAL KNOWLEDGE**

Adequacy of workforce is described through at least number and organizational knowledge; these are not interchangeable.

**STATUS: NEW / SINGLE-SOURCE**

**DIS-004 — WORKLOAD ≠ REQUIRED WORKFORCE**

Annex A.7.1.2 connects determination of necessary workforce to expected workload.

**STATUS: NEW / SINGLE-SOURCE**

**DIS-005 — PEOPLE ≠ IRREPLACEABLE MEANS OF FUNCTION**

Emerging technologies can be used in place of people for some functions. The Source therefore allows the function to be realized by a non-human technological means, subject to evaluation of related risks and opportunities.

**STATUS: NEW / SINGLE-SOURCE**

**DIS-006 — TECHNOLOGY SUBSTITUTION ≠ TECHNOLOGY ADOPTION WITHOUT CONSEQUENCE**

Replacing people with emerging technology requires evaluation of risks/opportunities and implications for ethical behaviour and accountability.

**STATUS: NEW / SINGLE-SOURCE**

### CMOC INTERPRETATION

7.1.2 makes the relationship between **function, required human resource and system capability** explicit:

```text
FUNCTION / PROCESS NEED
        ↓
REQUIRED CAPABILITY
        ↓
PEOPLE
        OR
TECHNOLOGICAL REALIZATION
        ↓
PROCESS OPERATION / CONTROL
        ↓
QMS RESULT
```

The important CMOC interpretation is:

> **People are not an organizational constant; the required human resource is determined by the functions and processes that the system must effectively operate and control.**

A second important interpretation:

> **Replacing a person with technology is a change in the realization of a function, not merely a change of resource.**

The latter is CMOC interpretation. The Source itself states that emerging technologies can be used in place of people for some functions and requires evaluation of associated risks/opportunities and ethical/accountability implications. fileciteturn59file5
