# PATCH-ISO-033

## Извещение на изменение

**0102+270826**

### SOURCE
ISO/DIS 9001:2025(en)

### LOCATION
Clause 8.5 — Production and service provision; 8.5.1–8.5.6; Annex A.8.5

### SOURCE CLAIM

Production and service provision shall be implemented under controlled conditions. Applicable controlled conditions include documented information defining product/service characteristics, activities to be performed and results to be achieved; suitable monitoring and measuring resources; monitoring and measurement at appropriate stages; suitable infrastructure and environment; competent persons; validation and periodic revalidation where output cannot be verified by subsequent monitoring or measurement; actions to prevent human error; and release, delivery and post-delivery activities. fileciteturn87file4

Outputs shall be identified where necessary to ensure conformity; their status with respect to monitoring and measurement requirements shall be identified throughout production and service provision; and unique identification shall be controlled when traceability is required, with necessary documented information available as evidence. fileciteturn87file14

Customer or external-provider property under the organization's control or used by it shall be identified, verified, protected and safeguarded. If lost, damaged or unsuitable, the relevant party shall be informed and documented information shall be available as evidence of what occurred. Preservation of outputs is required to the extent necessary to ensure conformity and can include identification, handling, contamination control, packaging, storage, transmission/transportation and protection. fileciteturn87file14

Post-delivery activities shall meet applicable requirements; their extent is determined considering statutory/regulatory requirements, potential undesired consequences, nature/use/intended lifetime, customer requirements and feedback. Production/service changes shall be reviewed and controlled as necessary to ensure continuing conformity; documented information shall evidence review results, authorization and necessary actions. fileciteturn87file14

Annex A.8.5 describes production and service delivery as core realization processes whose output directly affects customer satisfaction. Where output cannot be fully verified before delivery, process validation can supplement or replace output verification; detailed documented information and competence can provide confidence that the actual process is identical to the validated one and that output will conform. The organization determines the appropriate combination of verification and validation. At different realization stages, the state of control of output should be determinable. fileciteturn87file9

### DISTINCTIONS

**DIS-001 — CONTROLLED CONDITIONS ≠ SINGLE CONTROL**

Controlled conditions are a configuration of several applicable elements: information, resources, measurement, infrastructure/environment, competence, validation, error prevention and release/delivery/post-delivery activities.

**STATUS: NEW / SINGLE-SOURCE**

**DIS-002 — PROCESS VALIDATION ≠ OUTPUT VERIFICATION**

Validation concerns the ability of the process to achieve planned results when the resulting output cannot be verified by subsequent monitoring or measurement; it can supplement or replace output verification as appropriate.

**STATUS: STRONG CANDIDATE / SINGLE-SOURCE**

**DIS-003 — IDENTIFICATION ≠ TRACEABILITY**

Identification establishes suitable means for recognizing outputs; traceability additionally requires unique identification when traceability is a requirement.

**STATUS: NEW / SINGLE-SOURCE**

**DIS-004 — OUTPUT IDENTIFICATION ≠ OUTPUT STATUS**

Identification of an output and indication of its status with respect to monitoring/measurement are separate requirements.

**STATUS: NEW / SINGLE-SOURCE**

**DIS-005 — TRACEABILITY ≠ IDENTIFICATION ONLY**

Traceability requires controlled unique identification and available documented information necessary to enable tracing.

**STATUS: NEW / SINGLE-SOURCE**

**DIS-006 — CUSTOMER/EXTERNAL-PROVIDER PROPERTY ≠ ORGANIZATIONAL PROPERTY**

Property belonging to another party remains subject to defined care, identification, verification, protection and safeguarding while under organizational control/use.

**STATUS: NEW / SINGLE-SOURCE**

**DIS-007 — PRESERVATION ≠ STORAGE ONLY**

Preservation may include identification, handling, contamination control, packaging, storage, transmission/transportation and protection.

**STATUS: NEW / SINGLE-SOURCE**

**DIS-008 — PRODUCTION/SERVICE PROVISION ≠ DELIVERY**

Delivery is one element among production/service realization, not the whole realization process.

**STATUS: NEW / SINGLE-SOURCE**

**DIS-009 — PRODUCTION/SERVICE PROVISION ≠ POST-DELIVERY**

Post-delivery activities may remain part of applicable QMS obligations after delivery/provision.

**STATUS: NEW / SINGLE-SOURCE**

**DIS-010 — PLANNED PROCESS STATE ≠ ACTUAL STATE OF CONTROL**

The organization needs to be able to determine whether intermediate/final outputs have undergone planned controls and what the result was. fileciteturn87file9

**STATUS: STRONG CANDIDATE / SINGLE-SOURCE**

**DIS-011 — CHANGE ≠ UNCONTROLLED DEVIATION**

Changes in production/service provision are permissible when reviewed and controlled sufficiently to maintain continuing conformity.

**STATUS: NEW / SINGLE-SOURCE**

**DIS-012 — PROCESS COMPETENCE ≠ PROCESS VALIDATION**

Competent persons and validation are separate controlled-condition elements, although both can contribute to confidence in process results.

**STATUS: NEW / SINGLE-SOURCE**

### GM

**GM-001**

> Управляй производством/оказанием услуги через конфигурацию controlled conditions, а не через один контрольный пункт.

**GM-002**

> Если результат нельзя надёжно проверить после процесса, управляй способностью самого процесса стабильно достигать planned results через validation и revalidation.

**GM-003**

> Разделяй идентификацию output, его status и traceability.

**GM-004**

> Сохраняй output на всех необходимых стадиях так, чтобы preservation поддерживала conformity.

**GM-005**

> Для любого изменения production/service provision проверяй последствия для continuing conformity и фиксируй решение, authorization и необходимые actions.

### CAPABILITY

**CAP-033 — Controlled production/service provision capability**

CMOC interpretation: ability to realize products/services under configured and monitored conditions, determine the state of control of outputs, preserve conformity through realization, validate processes where output verification is insufficient, and control changes without losing continuing conformity.

**STATUS: CANDIDATE / SINGLE-SOURCE**

### CORE CANDIDATES

**CORE-CANDIDATE-080 — Controlled conditions are a system configuration**

Operational control can consist of a coordinated configuration of information, resources, measurements, environment, competence, validation, error prevention and release/delivery activities.

**STATUS: STRONG CANDIDATE / SINGLE-SOURCE**

**CORE-CANDIDATE-081 — Process can be the object of conformity assurance**

When output cannot be fully verified, assurance shifts partly from checking the output to validating and maintaining the process capability to achieve planned results.

**STATUS: STRONG CANDIDATE / SINGLE-SOURCE**

**CORE-CANDIDATE-082 — Output has a state of control**

During realization it is important to know whether an output has undergone planned control and what the result was.

**STATUS: STRONG CANDIDATE / SINGLE-SOURCE**

**CORE-CANDIDATE-083 — Identification, status and traceability are different control functions**

The clause separates recognizing an output, knowing its control status and uniquely tracing it when required.

**STATUS: STRONG CANDIDATE / SINGLE-SOURCE**

**CORE-CANDIDATE-084 — Preservation maintains conformity through transformation/handling**

Preservation is not merely storage; it is protection of output characteristics against adverse effects during realization and movement.

**STATUS: CANDIDATE / SINGLE-SOURCE**

**CORE-CANDIDATE-085 — Validation can replace output verification where verification is not practicable**

The Source explicitly allows validation to supplement or replace output verification, with the organization determining the appropriate combination.

**STATUS: STRONG CANDIDATE / SINGLE-SOURCE**

### DOCUMENT / DOCUMENTED INFORMATION / RECORD

8.5 materially strengthens the ISO-028 distinction.

Documented information is used for different functions:

```text
DOCUMENTATION
→ characteristics / activities / results / process information

EVIDENCE / RECORD-LIKE FUNCTION
→ traceability
→ customer/provider property incident
→ change review / authorization / actions
```

The Source explicitly requires documented information as evidence in several cases. It does not justify collapsing all documented information into Records.

### CMOC INTERPRETATION

The strongest new architecture is:

```text
REQUIREMENT
    ↓
CONTROLLED CONDITIONS
    ↓
PROCESS
    ↓
OUTPUT
    ↓
STATE OF CONTROL
    ↓
RELEASE
```

The critical insight is that **control is distributed across the realization process** rather than concentrated at the end.

A second strong interpretation:

> **Output becomes an управляемый объект не только через его характеристики, но и через знание его текущего состояния контроля.**

A third:

> **Если результат нельзя надёжно проверить после выполнения процесса, объектом обеспечения conformity становится способность самого процесса достигать planned results.**

This is CMOC interpretation based on Annex A.8.5. fileciteturn87file9
