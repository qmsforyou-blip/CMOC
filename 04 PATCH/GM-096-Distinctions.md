# GM-096 — Distinctions extracted for CMOC

**Source:** GM Quality Systems Basics rev. March 2009
**Basis:** SOURCE-FINAL-GM-QSB-2009.md and page-by-page extraction
**Status:** source-derived candidate distinctions; not yet Canon

## Purpose

This file separates the distinctions discovered in GM QSB from the mechanisms and Machines that may later realize them. The formulations below are CMOC-oriented abstractions grounded in the source; they are not presented as quotations from GM.

## Core distinctions

### D-01 — Requirement ≠ Document

A requirement and the document that carries, communicates or controls it are different objects.

**GM basis:** SHALL requirements are repeatedly translated into procedures, work instructions, forms, tracking and communication mechanisms.

### D-02 — Compliance ≠ Capability

Meeting a current requirement does not by itself prove that the organization or supplier possesses a developed, stable and improving capability.

**GM basis:** supplier assessment combines requirement compliance with capability/development assessment and best-practice gaps.

### D-03 — Detection ≠ Control

Detecting an abnormality does not by itself control its source or prevent recurrence.

**GM basis:** Fast Response, Contamination Control and problem-solving requirements repeatedly separate detection from source elimination, corrective action and system update.

### D-04 — Result ≠ Process

Verification of an output does not replace control of the process that produces it.

**GM basis:** QSB combines product verification with standardized operations, process monitoring, PFMEA, Control Plans, PM and audits.

### D-05 — Approval ≠ Authorization

A decision that a proposed change is acceptable is distinct from the written direction that permits its execution.

**GM basis:** PPCR flow separates review/approval from written direction to proceed.

### D-06 — Authorization ≠ Implementation

Permission to execute a change is distinct from the fact that the change has actually been implemented.

**GM basis:** PPCR requires the actual implementation date to be recorded separately.

### D-07 — Implementation ≠ Verification

Completion of an implementation does not by itself prove that the changed process performs acceptably.

**GM basis:** Production Trial Run and evaluation provide a separate verification path.

### D-08 — Implementation ≠ Final Approval

A completed change is not automatically a closed and finally accepted change record.

**GM basis:** final approval follows implementation and completion of required documentation/worksheets.

### D-09 — Temporary Deviation ≠ Uncontrolled Exception

A temporary departure from the normal process can be represented as a defined, bounded and controlled process state.

**GM basis:** Bypass / Backup Process Control requires defined methods, controls, entry/exit breakpoints and records.

### D-10 — Bypass ≠ Abandonment of Control

When the normal process is bypassed, control requirements are reconfigured for the bypass state rather than removed.

**GM basis:** bypass processes retain PFMEA, Control Plan, Standardized Work, inspection/audit and return controls.

### D-11 — Problem ≠ Corrective Action

The existence of an action does not prove that the systemic cause of the problem has been removed.

**GM basis:** problem-solving and Drill Deep & Wide require investigation into planning, process and control-system causes.

### D-12 — Workshop ≠ Training

A Workshop is not merely a knowledge-transfer event; it can be a mechanism for converting requirements and gaps into proposals, responsibilities and action plans.

**GM basis:** Workshop teams assess SHALL requirements, develop forms/tracking/review proposals and transfer unfinished work into Action Plans.

### D-13 — Supplier ≠ Purchase Source

A supplier is not treated merely as a source from which a purchased item is obtained; it is a capability within an extended quality system.

**GM basis:** supplier assessment, performance monitoring, development and cascading requirements to lower tiers.

### D-14 — Change ≠ New Accepted State

The decision or act of changing a process is not the same as establishing and accepting the resulting controlled state.

**GM basis:** PPCR approval, authorization, implementation, record, PTR/evaluation where required and final approval are distinct stages.

### D-15 — Measurement ≠ Control

Measurement creates evidence about a state; control additionally requires criteria, interpretation and a response path.

**GM basis:** contamination and supplier monitoring connect measurements to limits/status, reaction plans, corrective action and management review.

### D-16 — Record ≠ Evidence of Effectiveness

A record that an activity occurred is distinct from evidence that the intended result or capability was achieved.

**GM basis:** implementation records are followed, where applicable, by PTR, evaluation, verification and final approval.

### D-17 — State ≠ Status Record

A real process/product state and the recorded representation of that state are distinct objects that must remain linked.

**GM basis:** implementation dates, breakpoints, supplier metrics, R/Y/G status and audit records represent operational states without being identical to them.

### D-18 — Control Means ≠ Controlled State

The existence of a control measure does not establish that the target state is actually being maintained; the means of control may itself require verification and maintenance.

**GM basis:** contamination controls include functionality checks and preventive maintenance in addition to cleanliness requirements.

## Canonization rule

No distinction in this file is yet canonical merely because GM supports it. Before Canonization each candidate must be checked against the existing CMOC ontology for:

- duplication;
- scope;
- necessary/sufficient formulation;
- relation to existing entities and properties;
- provenance;
- whether the distinction belongs at the Entity, Property, Relation, Mechanism or Capability level.

**Next step:** map these distinctions to CMOC Mechanisms, then identify Machine candidates. Do not rewrite REG-001 in this step.
