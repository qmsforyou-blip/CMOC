## Historical extraction

The Workshop section shows a mechanism for translating QSB requirements and identified gaps into organizational action.

The Workshop is organized around cross-functional teams. Team composition is aligned with the strategy and the competencies needed to change the relevant process. Results are presented to Top Management. The presentation is a starting point for further development rather than the final implementation itself.

Unfinished items at the closing meeting are transferred into the QSB Action Plan. Thus an unresolved gap does not disappear when the Workshop ends; it receives a further management path.

The team is expected to examine each SHALL not merely as a statement, but through its implementation architecture:

`SHALL → Current Status → Form/Record → Tracking → Analysis → Management Review → Communication → Action Plan`

The Workshop therefore operates as a conversion mechanism:

`Requirement → Assessment → Status/Gap → Proposal → Action → Responsibility → Tracking → Management Review → Closure`

## Historical analytical conclusion

The key conclusion reached during the working session was:

> **Workshop is a mechanism for collective transition from a requirement and identified gap to a controlled organizational action.**

And:

> **A diagnosis or review is not complete merely because a gap has been identified; the gap must acquire an action, responsibility and tracking mechanism.**

## CMOC significance

This is not to be canonized automatically as a Machine. It is better treated initially as a mechanism / control pattern for converting requirements and diagnostic findings into managed action.

The finding is particularly relevant to CMOC diagnostic architecture and to the future design of diagnostic products, because it provides a source-derived industrial example of the chain:

`Requirement → Assessment → Gap → Action → Responsibility → Tracking → Management Review`
